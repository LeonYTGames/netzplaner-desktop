Help & Shortcuts

Netzplaner is fully manual. Values like FAZ, FEZ, SAZ, SEZ, FP and GP are never calculated automatically. Connections are purely visual.

Usage

Nodes can be moved by drag and drop. If the grid is active and 'Snap to grid' is enabled, positions snap to grid points. For connections, activate Connection first, then click the start and target nodes. Auto-sort arranges the nodes into columns that follow their connections; one undo step restores the previous positions.
